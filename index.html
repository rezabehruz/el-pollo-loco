Welcome

El Pollo Loco is a 2D platform action game where you must defeat dangerous chickens, collect coins and salsa bottles, and overcome obstacles to reach the final boss.

Objective

Your mission is to explore the level, collect as many coins and salsa bottles as possible, avoid or defeat enemies, and defeat the final boss to win the game.

Controls

Action Key
Move Left ← Left Arrow
Move Right → Right Arrow
Jump Space
Throw Salsa Bottle D
